# SMP-Social 1.0.0 (author: Kaloyan)
`/msg <player> <message>` (aliases /tell /w /m /t) and `/reply` (/r); `/ignore <player>` hides that player's chat and blocks their messages (again to stop, `/ignore list`);
`/trash` (a bin: items are deleted when you close it), `/afk` and automatic away after 5 minutes without looking around, chatting, clicking or building (walking or being pushed by water does not count, so
AFK pools do not work). Away players get no shards (SMP-Shop) and no playing time (SMP-Stats). Vanished staff cannot be messaged. Data: `ignores.yml`.

Private messages are never logged and nobody else can read them (there is deliberately no social spy).
