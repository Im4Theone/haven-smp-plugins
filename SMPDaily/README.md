# SMP-Daily 1.0.0 (author: im4theone)
`/daily` (aliases /rewards) opens a 7-day calendar. One reward every 24 hours; claim again within 48 hours to keep the streak, miss a day and it starts again at day 1; after day 7
it starts again. Each day gives money, shards and/or crates (config.yml). A reminder shows on join when a reward is waiting. The claim is saved first, then paid; if the money
cannot be paid the claim is undone. Needs Vault (SMP-Economy); shards need SMP-Shop and crates SMP-Crates.
